# Real plan images and their licences

Plans drawn by other people, used to measure the raster reader on drawings we
did not generate. Keep this file in step with the fixtures.

## putnam-house-habs.png

- Drawing: General Israel Putnam House, Danvers, Essex County, Massachusetts,
  sheet 1 of 9, first floor plan. Hand lettered, surveyed with Massachusetts
  E.R.A. funds, delineated by Bernard Boisclair, built 1650 to 1744.
- Source: Historic American Buildings Survey (HABS MASS,5-DANV,3-), Library of
  Congress, via Wikimedia Commons:
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Putnam_House_-_floor_plans.jpg
- Licence: public domain. A work of the United States federal government
  (Title 17, Chapter 1, Section 105 of the US Code), also marked with the
  Creative Commons Public Domain Mark 1.0.
- What we did: the full sheet is 8199 x 6607 pixels and carries two floor
  plans, a door schedule and a title block. The first floor plan was cropped
  and scaled to 1557 x 1600 pixels, the size a reader model sees. Nothing else
  was changed: the noise, hand lettering, dimension chains and hatching are as
  scanned.
