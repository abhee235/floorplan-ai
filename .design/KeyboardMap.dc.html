Keyboard and focus

Every gesture has a keyboard equal. One tab stop per region; arrows move within a region. The canvas is one stop, entered with Enter and left with Escape.

Focus ring: 2 px #1e6fd9 with a 2 px offset, on every control and on the canvas entity in focus. Targets 32 px on a pointer, 44 px on touch. Text and icons meet WCAG 2.2 AA against their own background; motion respects reduced-motion.
